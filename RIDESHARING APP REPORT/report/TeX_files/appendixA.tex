\hline
\textbf{Requirement Description} & \textbf{User Roles and Profiles} --- Users shall be able to maintain a profile and interact with the system as a driver or passenger. The system shall allow an authenticated user to operate as a driver or passenger and shall store the relevant profile information associated with the user's account and role. \\
\hline
\textbf{Non-functional Requirements} & NFR01 Security --- role-based access protection; \newline NFR02 Reliability --- profile and role data stored without inconsistency. \\

\textbf{Requirement ID} & RQ03 \\
\hline
\textbf{Requirement Description} & \textbf{Driver Ride Posting} --- Drivers shall be able to offer available seats on a planned journey. The system shall allow drivers to create and publish a ride offer by entering their origin, destination, departure time and number of available seats. The ride offer shall be stored and made available for matching and searching. \\
\hline
\textbf{Non-functional Requirements} & NFR02 Reliability --- consistent seat availability data; \newline NFR03 Performance --- ride offers processed within a reasonable time. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\hline
\textbf{Requirement ID} & RQ04 \\
\hline
\textbf{Requirement Description} & \textbf{Passenger Ride Request} --- Passengers shall be able to request a ride for a planned journey. The system shall allow passengers to submit a ride request containing their origin, destination and relevant travel information. The request shall be stored and made available for matching suitable ride offers. \\
\hline
\textbf{Non-functional Requirements} & NFR02 Reliability --- accurate request data handling; \newline NFR03 Performance --- timely request submission and storage. \\
\hline

\hline
\textbf{Requirement ID} & RQ05 \\
\hline
\textbf{Requirement Description} & \textbf{Ride Matching} --- Users shall be able to identify rides that are compatible with their journeys. The system shall compare relevant driver and passenger journey information, including origin, destination, departure time, available seating and route compatibility, and shall identify potential compatible rides according to the implemented matching criteria. \\
\hline
\textbf{Non-functional Requirements} & NFR03 Performance --- matches generated within a reasonable period; \newline NFR04 Location accuracy --- accurate route/location comparison; \newline NFR05 Scalability --- matching supports increasing rides/requests. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{minipage}
