RQ01 & \textbf{User Registration and Authentication} --- Users shall be able to create and access an account. The system shall allow users to register using the required account information and authenticate their identity before accessing protected functionality. Authentication credentials shall be securely handled by the authentication service. & NFR01 Security --- authentication credentials secured through appropriate access controls. \\
\hline

RQ02 & \textbf{User Roles and Profiles} --- Users shall be able to maintain a profile and use the system as a driver or passenger. The system shall allow an authenticated user to operate as a driver or passenger and shall store the relevant profile information associated with the user's account and role. & NFR02 Reliability --- profile and role data stored without inconsistency; \newline NFR01 Security --- role-based access protection. \\
\hline

RQ03 & \textbf{Driver Ride Posting} --- Drivers shall be able to offer available seats on a planned journey. The system shall allow drivers to create and publish a ride offer by entering their origin, destination, departure time and number of available seats. The ride offer shall be stored and made available for matching and searching. & NFR03 Performance --- ride offers processed within a reasonable time; \newline NFR02 Reliability --- consistent seat availability data. \\
\hline

RQ04 & \textbf{Passenger Ride Request} --- Passengers shall be able to request a ride for a planned journey. The system shall allow passengers to submit a ride request containing their origin, destination and relevant travel information. The request shall be stored and made available for matching against suitable ride offers. & NFR03 Performance --- timely request submission and storage; \newline NFR02 Reliability --- accurate request data handling. \\
\hline

RQ05 & \textbf{Ride Matching} --- Users shall be able to identify rides that are compatible with their journeys. The system shall compare relevant driver and passenger journey information, including origin, destination, departure time, available seating and route compatibility, and shall identify potential compatible rides according to the implemented matching criteria. & NFR03 Performance --- matches generated within a reasonable period; \newline NFR04 Location accuracy --- accurate route/location comparison; \newline NFR05 Scalability --- matching supports increasing rides/requests. \\
\hline

RQ06 & \textbf{Ride Request Management} --- Drivers shall be able to respond to passenger requests. The system shall allow drivers to view requests associated with their ride offers and accept or decline individual requests. The corresponding request status shall be updated and stored. & NFR06 Usability --- drivers can respond with minimal difficulty; \newline NFR02 Reliability --- status updates stored consistently. \\
\hline

RQ07 & \textbf{Ride Status Management} --- Users shall be able to know the current status of a ride or request. The system shall maintain and update ride and request statuses, including relevant states such as requested, accepted, completed and cancelled. Updated statuses shall be available to the relevant users. & NFR02 Reliability --- statuses updated without data loss or inconsistency. \\
\hline

RQ08 & \textbf{Ride History} --- Users shall be able to view their previous rides and requests. The system shall store records of relevant ride offers, ride requests and completed rides and shall allow authenticated users to retrieve and view their own ride history. & NFR02 Reliability --- historical records retained accurately. \\
\hline

RQ09 & \textbf{Interactive Map} --- Users shall be able to view and select locations associated with their journeys. The system shall provide an interactive map through which users can select or view relevant origin and destination locations. The selected location information shall be associated with the relevant ride offer or request. & NFR04 Location accuracy --- accurate locations/routes from mapping services; \newline NFR06 Usability --- simple location selection. \\
\hline

RQ10 & \textbf{Notifications} --- Users shall receive notifications about relevant ride events. The system shall notify relevant users when events occur, including new ride requests, accepted or declined requests and relevant ride-status changes. & NFR07 Availability --- notifications delivered while core services are accessible; \newline NFR03 Performance --- timely delivery. \\
\hline

RQ11 & \textbf{Ride Search and Filtering} --- Passengers shall be able to search for available rides that meet their travel requirements. The system shall allow passengers to search available ride offers and apply relevant criteria, such as destination, departure time and available seats, to narrow the displayed results. & NFR03 Performance --- search results returned promptly; \newline NFR06 Usability --- filtering with minimal difficulty. \\
\hline

RQ12 & \textbf{Data Storage and Retrieval} --- The system shall retain the information required to operate the ride-sharing service. The system shall store and retrieve user profiles, ride offers, ride requests, ride statuses, seating information and ride histories using the project's database. & NFR02 Reliability --- data stored without loss or inconsistency; \newline NFR05 Scalability --- database supports growing data volume. \\
\hline

RQ13 & \textbf{Access Control} --- Users shall only access functionality and information appropriate to their account. The system shall restrict protected functionality and user-specific information according to the authenticated user's account and role. & NFR01 Security --- enforced through authentication and access controls. \\
\hline

RQ14 & \textbf{Mobile Application Support} --- Users shall be able to access the ride-sharing system through supported mobile devices. The system shall provide its core ride-sharing functionality through the developed mobile application on the supported Android and iOS platforms. & NFR07 Availability --- core functionality accessible on supported platforms; \newline NFR06 Usability --- consistent, easy-to-use mobile interface. \\
\hline

RQ15 & \textbf{Ride Matching Results} --- Users shall receive the results of the ride-matching process. The system shall process submitted driver and passenger journey information and return potential compatible ride matches based on the implemented matching criteria. & NFR03 Performance --- results generated within a reasonable period; \newline NFR04 Location accuracy --- accurate matches based on location/route data. \\
\hline

RQ16 & \textbf{Core Task Completion} --- Users shall be able to complete the core tasks required to use the ride-sharing system. The system shall provide interfaces that allow drivers and passengers to complete the defined core tasks. The usability of these interfaces shall be evaluated using the System Usability Scale (SUS). & NFR06 Usability --- evaluated via the System Usability Scale (SUS). \\
\hline

\end{longtable}
